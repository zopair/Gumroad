# Zubair Digital Kits — Product Catalog

Original, low-dependency digital workbooks and templates for independent operators and small businesses.

## Product principles
- Original text and workflows; no copied competitor assets.
- No external scripts, browser extensions, API keys, telemetry, or paid dependencies.
- No fabricated testimonials, sales claims, customer data, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Each product includes a clear scope and usage limitations.
- Product files are Markdown-first and can be used in any text editor or copied into compatible office tools.

Revisit after real page-view, conversion, refund, and support data exists. Do not infer demand from price alone.
