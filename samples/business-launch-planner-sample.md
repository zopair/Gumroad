# Free Preview — Business Launch Planner

This sample uses a fictional neighborhood meal-prep service. The example is illustrative only.

## One-page concept snapshot

- Customer segment: busy professionals within a 3 km delivery radius.
- Problem to test: weekday meal planning takes too much time.
- Current alternative: takeaway meals and batch cooking on weekends.
- First offer to test: a prepaid three-meal trial box.
- Main assumption: customers value predictable delivery more than a wide menu.
