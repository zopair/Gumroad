# Free Preview — Caption Writing Workbench

## Five-part sample

- Opening: Name one specific audience problem.
- Context: Explain why the problem appears in this situation.
- Value: Give one useful method or decision rule.
- Proof: Add a real example or verifiable source.
- Action: Ask for one relevant next step.

## Example opening

"Your project estimate is probably missing one quiet cost: the time spent clarifying requirements."

The full workbench includes 12 adaptable patterns, platform guidance, draft worksheets and a final quality gate.
