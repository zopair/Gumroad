# Free Preview — Client Onboarding Pack

## Sample intake questions

1. What business outcome should this project support?
2. Who will approve the final deliverable?
3. Which files, tools or dependencies must be available before work begins?

## Sample kickoff checks

- Confirm the approved scope and exclusions.
- Agree review dates and the decision owner.
- Grant named, least-privilege access with an expiry date.

The full pack includes welcome copy, intake questionnaire, kickoff agenda, responsibility matrix, access checklist and handoff checklist.
