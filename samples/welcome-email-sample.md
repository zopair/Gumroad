# Free Preview — Welcome Email Sequence Kit

## Email 1 — Deliver the promise

**Purpose:** Give the subscriber exactly what the signup form promised.

**Subject option:** Your requested guide is ready

**Opening:** Thanks for requesting the guide. You can access it here: [insert your verified link].

**Body framework:**
- Confirm what the subscriber requested.
- Explain one simple way to use it today.
- State what they can expect next and how often you will write.
- Include the required unsubscribe link and sender details.

The full kit includes five email frameworks, subject options, suggested send intervals and a consent-focused checklist.
