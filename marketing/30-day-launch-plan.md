# 30-Day Organic Store Launch Plan

Goal: generate the first qualified visits and validate which of the ten live products has buyer demand. This is a distribution plan, not a promise of sales.

## Channel roles

- Pinterest: evergreen discovery for templates, planners and worksheets. Use the ten vertical PNG pins in this folder.
- LinkedIn: demonstrate one practical business workflow and link only when the product is directly relevant.
- Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts: show a real workbook task with a screen recording, captions and one CTA.
- Gumroad profile: convert visitors with exact file lists, product previews and FAQs.

## Posting cadence

- Pinterest: publish one new pin per day for 10 days, then repin with a different useful angle after 7–10 days.
- LinkedIn: 3 useful posts per week; no repetitive sales-only posts.
- Reels / Shorts: 2 demonstrations per week, starting with the CRM, pricing calculator, content calendar and launch planner.
- Review Gumroad analytics once a week and record source, product views, paid sales and conversion rate.

## UTM tracking

Use a distinct URL for each channel and product, for example:

- Pinterest: https://zopair.gumroad.com/l/30-day-social-media-content-calendar?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=30day_calendar
- LinkedIn: https://zopair.gumroad.com/l/simple-crm-lead-tracker?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=crm_tracker
- Short video: https://zopair.gumroad.com/l/service-pricing-calculator?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=pricing_demo

Do not use the same UTM campaign for unrelated products. Record the final URLs in the campaign tracker.

## Weekly review

1. Which product received qualified views?
2. Which source produced those views?
3. Did visitors reach the product page and purchase?
4. Which image or description angle was used?
5. What question or objection appeared?
6. What single change will be tested next week?

If views remain zero, fix distribution and discoverability before changing prices. If views rise but sales remain zero, inspect product-market fit, deliverable clarity, preview quality and trust. If sales occur, inspect buyer questions and refund reasons before scaling.
