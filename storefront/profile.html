Before you buy

Frequently asked questions

What do I receive after purchase?

Each product includes an editable Markdown guide, a matching CSV worksheet and an Excel workbook. The product page lists the exact workbook features and limitations.

Do these products require a subscription?

No subscription is required for the listed one-time digital downloads.

Are these standalone apps?

No. These are templates, guides and planning tools. They do not include hosted software, automated publishing, CRM services or email-sending platforms.

Can I use the files in Excel or Google Sheets?

CSV worksheets and XLSX workbooks can be opened in Microsoft Excel and compatible spreadsheet applications. Check each product page for specific compatibility notes.

Do the templates guarantee business or marketing results?

No. They provide structure and practical prompts. Results depend on your audience, offer, execution, evidence and distribution.

What if I cannot open a download?

Use the contact option on the relevant product page and include the product name and a brief description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.
