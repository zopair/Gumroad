Before you buy

Frequently asked questions

What do I receive after purchase?

Each product page lists its exact files. Each current kit includes three files: an editable Markdown guide, a matching CSV worksheet and an Excel workbook. The product page lists the exact workbook features and compatibility notes.

Do these products require a subscription?

No subscription is required for the listed one-time digital downloads. Any product-specific access conditions are shown on its own page.

Are these standalone apps?

No. These are templates, guides and planning tools. They do not include hosted software, automated publishing, CRM services or email-sending platforms unless a product page explicitly says otherwise.

Can I use the files in Excel or Google Sheets?

The included CSV worksheets and Excel workbooks can be opened in Microsoft Excel and compatible spreadsheet applications. Check each product page for the precise file formats.

Do the templates guarantee business or marketing results?

No. They provide structure and practical prompts. Results depend on your audience, offer, execution, evidence and distribution.

What if I cannot open a download?

Use the contact option on the relevant product page and include the product name and a brief description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.
