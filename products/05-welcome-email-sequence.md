# Welcome Email Sequence Kit

Use this sequence for subscribers who explicitly opted in. Follow applicable email, privacy, and consent rules. Do not add purchased or scraped addresses.

## Email 1 — Deliver the promise
**Subject options:** Your [resource] is here / Start here: [resource]

Hi [first name],

Here is the [resource] you requested: [link].

Start with [first action]. It should help you [specific practical purpose]. If you get stuck, reply and tell me where.

You can expect [accurate description of future emails]. You can unsubscribe at any time.

[Name]

## Email 2 — Understand the problem
**Subject options:** A common obstacle with [topic] / Before you try [task]

Hi [first name],

A common reason [task] becomes difficult is [specific cause]. A useful first check is [step].

Try this:
1. [Action]
2. [Action]
3. [Review]

The goal is not perfection; it is to identify the next useful improvement.

[Name]

## Email 3 — Teach one method
**Subject options:** A simple framework for [outcome] / Try this 3-step method

Hi [first name],

Use this framework:
- Define [input]
- Choose [decision]
- Review [output]

Example: [realistic example clearly marked as illustrative].

If you want to go deeper, [relevant resource / article].

[Name]

## Email 4 — Address a real objection
**Subject options:** Is [approach] right for you? / What if you have limited time?

Hi [first name],

You may be wondering [real objection]. The honest answer is [nuanced response].

This approach is useful when [fit]. It is not ideal when [non-fit]. If you are in the first group, start with [small action].

[Name]

## Email 5 — Invite a next step
**Subject options:** Your next step with [topic] / A resource for [specific task]

Hi [first name],

If you are ready to [outcome], here is [offer/resource]: [link].

It includes [specific deliverables], is designed for [audience], and does not include [exclusions].

Review the details and decide whether it fits your needs.

[Name]

## Sequence planning
| Email | Suggested delay | Purpose | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediately | Deliver requested item | Delivery / clicks |
| 2 | 2 days | Clarify problem | Replies |
| 3 | 2 days later | Teach | Clicks / saves |
| 4 | 3 days later | Resolve objection | Replies |
| 5 | 3 days later | Invite next step | Qualified clicks / conversions |

## Compliance and QA
- [ ] Explicit consent captured
- [ ] Sender identity and contact details present
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism works
- [ ] Links and personalization tested
- [ ] No unsupported claims or fake scarcity
- [ ] Suppression list respected
- [ ] Frequency matches signup promise
