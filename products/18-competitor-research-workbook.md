## Review mining worksheet
For each public review, record:
- Date and source
- Buyer use case
- What worked
- What was missing
- Whether the issue is repeated
- Whether the complaint is relevant to our intended audience

Do not infer sales from reviews unless the source explicitly provides that data.

## Opportunity statement
For [specific buyer] who needs [job], existing options [observed limitation]. We can offer [original approach] with [verifiable differentiator]. We will test this by [small validation experiment].

## Evidence quality
- A: direct public product facts or seller-disclosed metrics
- B: independent third-party estimate with stated method
- C: indirect signal such as review count or social engagement
- D: unverified claim or assumption

Keep C and D separate from actual sales.
