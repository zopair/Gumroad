## Intent categories
- Informational: learn or understand
- Commercial investigation: compare options
- Transactional: take an action or buy
- Navigational: reach a known brand or page

## Prioritization
Score each item 1–5:
- Business relevance
- Audience fit
- Ability to produce a genuinely useful answer
- Evidence of demand
- Competitive feasibility

Priority score = sum of the five scores. This is a triage aid, not a ranking prediction.

## Cannibalization check
- Search existing site pages for the same intent.
- Compare page purpose, not only exact keywords.
- Consolidate overlapping pages where one stronger resource serves the need.
- Keep separate pages only when intent or audience differs meaningfully.
