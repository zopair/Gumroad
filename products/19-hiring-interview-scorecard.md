Weighted score = sum(score × weight) ÷ sum(weights). Use the score as one input, not an automatic decision.

## Structured questions
1. Describe a relevant project and your specific contribution.
2. Walk through a difficult problem and the steps you took.
3. Tell us about a time requirements changed. What did you do?
4. How do you check the quality of your work?
5. What information would you need to succeed in this role?
