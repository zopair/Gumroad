# Caption Writing Workbench

## The 5-part caption structure
1. **Opening:** Name a specific situation or problem.
2. **Context:** Explain why it matters to this audience.
3. **Value:** Give one useful method, example, or decision rule.
4. **Proof:** Add a verifiable example, demonstration, or source.
5. **Action:** Ask for one relevant next step.

## 12 caption patterns
### 1. Problem → fix
If you are struggling with [specific problem], check [one factor]. Try [action]. You should then review [measurement] to see whether it helped.

### 2. Checklist
Before [task], check: [item 1], [item 2], [item 3]. Save this for the next time you do it.

### 3. Myth → nuance
It is common to hear [claim]. The missing context is [nuance]. In [situation A], use [approach]; in [situation B], consider [alternative].

### 4. Short tutorial
To [outcome]: first [step 1], then [step 2], finally [step 3]. The most important check is [quality check].

### 5. Comparison
Choose [A] when [condition]. Choose [B] when [condition]. Compare them on [criterion], not on [vanity metric].

### 6. Behind the scenes
The goal was [goal]. The constraint was [constraint]. I chose [decision] because [reason]. Next I will evaluate [metric].

### 7. FAQ
Question: [real customer question]. Short answer: [answer]. Important limitation: [limitation]. Next step: [action].

### 8. Case note
Situation: [context]. Action: [what was done]. Observed result: [verified result]. Lesson: [lesson]. Do not generalize beyond the evidence.

### 9. Product explanation
[Product] includes [deliverables]. It is designed for [audience/use case]. It does not include [exclusions]. See the preview before deciding.

### 10. Objection response
If your concern is [objection], consider [relevant fact]. This resource may fit if [condition], but may not fit if [condition].

### 11. Curated resources
For [task], start with [resource 1], [resource 2], and [resource 3]. I use each for [reason]. Verify current versions before relying on them.

### 12. Reflection
I used to assume [assumption]. Experience with [context] made me reconsider [lesson]. What I now do is [practice].

## Platform adaptation
| Platform | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Strong first line, readable spacing, visual context, one CTA |
| LinkedIn | Professional context, specific lesson, evidence, thoughtful question |
| X | One idea per post; split complex ideas into a thread |
| Facebook | Conversational context and community relevance |
| Newsletter | Subject line, useful opening, deeper explanation, one action |

## Final quality gate
- [ ] One audience, one problem, one CTA
- [ ] No fabricated statistics or testimonials
- [ ] Claims are sourced or clearly framed as opinion
- [ ] No misleading urgency
- [ ] Spelling and links checked
- [ ] Disclosure added where required
