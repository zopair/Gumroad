# Sticker & Colouring Pack — Original Characters Edition

<badge>Internal review build — not published</badge>

## Product summary

A digital pack of original-character artwork prepared as transparent PNG stickers, square colouring pages, and low-resolution original reference renders.

## Verified source inventory

- 18 transparent PNG sticker assets.
- 13 JPEG colouring pages, each 1600 × 1600 px.
- 31 original reference renders, each 400 × 400 px.
- One 1429 × 804 px cover.
- README, candidate license, listing copy, and image specification.
- 67 source files; 19,860,799 bytes before generated delivery files.

The original assets are retained without recompression. The source repository contains one nested sticker path that differs from the path in `spec.json`; the QA report records the normalization used for dimension validation.

## Customer delivery generated by GitHub Actions

- `delivery/sticker-colouring-pack.zip` — full source pack plus the print PDF.
- `delivery/colouring-pages.pdf` — 13 square pages, approximately 5.33 × 5.33 inches.
- `quality-report.md` — automated file-integrity and image checks.
- `manifest.json` — source byte sizes and Git blob SHA-1 fingerprints.

## Commercial draft

**Target price:** USD $39 for the single-user tier.

A 3-user tier at USD $100 appears in the source listing draft but remains unapproved. No final pricing decision is recorded until the product description, customer use rights, and marketplace policy review are complete.

## Disclosures

- Source README and listing state that artwork was AI-generated from 400 × 400 px JPEG renders and AI-upscaled.
- The source documents disclose possible small artifacts and note that sticker borders are tuned for light backgrounds.
- Colouring pages are style-matched editions, not exact tracings of the coloured character art.
- No professional-print guarantee is made.
- The license text is explicitly marked as a candidate for seller review; it is not approved for customer distribution yet.
- Legal ownership and rights to commercialize each AI-generated asset have not been independently established by the automated QA.
