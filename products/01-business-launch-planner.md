## 5. 14-day validation sprint
| Day | Action | Evidence to collect | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Define one customer segment | 10 target profiles | Segment is reachable |
| 3–5 | Conduct problem interviews | 5 detailed conversations | Repeated pain, not praise |
| 6–7 | Review alternatives | 5 competing offers | Clear gap or differentiation |
| 8–10 | Build smallest useful version | Working sample | User can complete one task |
| 11–12 | Offer a paid pilot | Price objections and commitments | At least one real commitment |
| 13–14 | Decide | Evidence summary | Continue, revise, or stop |

## 6. Go / revise / stop
- GO if: the same problem repeats, buyers already spend time or money on it, and a small solution is feasible.
- REVISE if: pain exists but segment, promise, or format is unclear.
- STOP if: interest is only polite, no workaround exists, and no one will commit.

## 7. Launch checklist
- [ ] Product works from a clean copy
- [ ] Instructions and limitations are included
- [ ] Price and refund terms are clear
- [ ] Product page describes exact deliverables
- [ ] Support contact is monitored
