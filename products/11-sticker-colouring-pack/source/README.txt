HOW TO USE
- Stickers: transparent PNGs with a white die-cut border, tuned for light backgrounds. For digital use, place directly on your design. For printing, order a sample first or consult your printer; sizes suit small formats (roughly 2-5 inches tall at 300 dpi depending on the design).
- Colouring pages: print at 100% for a ~5.3 inch square at 300 dpi, or scale to fit your page.

HONEST NOTES
- Artwork was AI-generated as 400x400 px JPEGs, then AI-upscaled. Small artifacts (edge noise, faint colour accents on some outlines) can show at high magnification, especially on dark backgrounds.
- Colouring pages are style-matched editions, not exact tracings of the coloured characters.
- Dimensions above are measured from the delivered files.
