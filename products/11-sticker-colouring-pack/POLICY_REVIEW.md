# Gumroad Policy Review — Sticker & Colouring Pack

**Review date:** 2026-11-22  
**Status:** Preliminary platform-policy review; not legal advice and not a platform pre-approval.

## 1. Product type

The deliverable is a static digital-art pack (PNG/JPEG/PDF/ZIP), not access to an AI tool, chatbot, image generator, or AI service. Gumroad's prohibited-products list names AI services, not all static artwork made with AI. The product copy must accurately disclose the source workflow already stated in the pack README: 400 × 400 px AI-generated JPEG renders were AI-upscaled.

## 2. Age-targeting restriction — material listing constraint

Gumroad's current Terms of Service, section 11.2(a)(v), prohibits products that target or intend to distribute to children under thirteen. Do not market this pack as a kids' product, children's activity pack, preschool material, or for an under-13 age range. Keep the positioning neutral: sticker assets, journaling, planners, personal craft projects, and colouring pages for a general/teen/adult audience. This is a marketplace rule, not a conclusion about the artwork's legality.

Official references:
- https://gumroad.com/terms
- https://gumroad.com/prohibited
- https://gumroad.com/help/article/155-things-you-cant-sell-on-gumroad

## 3. Satirical lettering design

The source pack includes the exact text: "I wasn't in the Epstein files either." The design is retained in the source and delivery files. The reviewed Gumroad policy pages prohibit targeted harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure of private information; the quoted sentence alone does not identify a private person, threaten anyone, or disclose private information. This is not a guarantee of platform acceptance: Gumroad reserves discretion over content it considers offensive or otherwise contrary to its Terms. Do not add unsupported factual allegations or identify private individuals in promotional copy.

## 4. Intellectual-property and authorship representation

Gumroad Terms section 13.3 requires the supplier to represent that they own, or have sufficient rights to use and commercially license, the submitted content. The pack's source README says the artwork was AI-generated and upscaled; automated image checks cannot establish copyright ownership, originality against third-party works, or the scope of rights in the source-generation service. Do not claim exclusive copyright, trademark rights, or human authorship without supporting evidence. Preserve prompts, generation records, source files, and any applicable tool terms as provenance evidence.

Official reference:
- https://gumroad.com/terms

## 5. Listing and delivery requirements

The pack contains actual downloadable files, so the product should attach the ZIP/PDF directly to Gumroad rather than make delivery depend only on an external message or contact. Keep all claims consistent with the verified manifest and quality report. Avoid claims of professional print quality or exact tracing; the source README explicitly discloses AI-upscaling artifacts and style-matched colouring pages.

## 6. Decision

- **Keep** the Epstein satirical lettering asset in the source pack.
- **Do not** position the product as aimed at children under 13.
- **Do not** claim exclusive ownership or guaranteed copyright protection without evidence.
- **Do not publish yet:** the existing Gumroad draft must be reconciled with the new ZIP/PDF, and the seller license remains a candidate pending approval.
