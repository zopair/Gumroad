## Kickoff agenda (30–45 minutes)
1. Confirm desired outcome and success criteria.
2. Confirm scope, exclusions and assumptions.
3. Confirm stakeholders and decision owner.
4. Review timeline and dependencies.
5. Agree feedback and approval process.
6. Confirm risks and escalation route.
7. Record decisions and owners.

## Access and security checklist
- [ ] Use least-privilege access
- [ ] Use named accounts; no shared passwords
- [ ] Enable MFA where available
- [ ] Transfer credentials through approved secret manager
- [ ] Define access expiry date
- [ ] Remove access at project close
- [ ] Avoid sending sensitive data in ordinary email

## Handoff checklist
- [ ] Deliverables match approved scope
- [ ] Files named and organized
- [ ] Usage instructions included
- [ ] Known limitations documented
- [ ] Client has confirmed receipt
- [ ] Support period and boundaries restated
- [ ] Access reviewed and unnecessary access removed
