Net movement = receipts + other inflows − payroll − suppliers − operating costs − tax/debt.  
Closing cash = opening cash + net movement.  
Next week's opening cash = prior week's closing cash.

## Risk flags
- Closing cash below minimum reserve
- One customer represents a large share of expected receipts
- Tax or debt payment not funded
- Receipts depend on unsigned proposals
- Forecast relies on overdue invoices

## Weekly review
1. Replace forecast values with actuals.
2. Move expected dates based on evidence.
3. Record reasons for variance.
4. Review the lowest projected cash point.
5. Assign an owner and action to every risk.
