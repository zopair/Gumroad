## Status definitions
- Backlog: not yet committed
- Ready: requirements and dependencies understood
- In progress: active work
- Blocked: cannot proceed; blocker and owner required
- Review: awaiting validation or approval
- Done: acceptance evidence recorded

## Weekly review
- What was completed and accepted?
- What is late and why?
- Which dependency is at risk?
- What decision is needed?
- What changed in scope?
- What will be delivered next week?

## Definition of done
- [ ] Requirements met
- [ ] Tests / review completed
- [ ] Documentation updated
- [ ] Owner accepts output
- [ ] Risks and limitations disclosed
- [ ] Handoff complete
