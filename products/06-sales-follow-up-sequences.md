# Sales Follow-Up Message Pack

Use these as starting points, not automated spam. Contact only people with a lawful basis or appropriate permission, honor opt-outs, and personalize truthfully.

## 1. Inbound inquiry — same day
**Subject:** Re: [their request]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out about [specific need]. To recommend the right option, I need to confirm [one or two essential details].

Based on what you shared, [brief relevant observation]. Would [proposed next step] be useful?

Regards,  
[Name]

## 2. After discovery call
**Subject:** Summary and next steps — [project]

Hi [Name],

Here is my understanding:
- Goal: [goal]
- Current constraint: [constraint]
- Priority: [priority]
- Open question: [question]

I will [your action] by [date]. You will [their action] by [date]. Please correct anything I have misunderstood.

Regards,  
[Name]

## 3. Proposal sent — after 2–3 business days
**Subject:** Checking the [project] proposal

Hi [Name],

I am checking whether the proposal answered your questions about [scope / timeline / investment]. If something is unclear, send me the section and I will clarify it.

If priorities have changed, that is useful to know too.

Regards,  
[Name]

## 4. Decision support
**Subject:** One point to help with the decision

Hi [Name],

One trade-off worth considering is [trade-off]. Option A is stronger when [condition]; option B is stronger when [condition].

If useful, I can revise the scope around [specific priority]. No pressure if the timing is not right.

Regards,  
[Name]

## 5. Close the loop
**Subject:** Should I close this request?

Hi [Name],

I have not heard back, so I will close the loop for now and avoid filling your inbox. If [project] becomes a priority later, you can reach me at [contact].

Thanks,  
[Name]

## 6. Existing customer — check-in
**Subject:** How is [deliverable] working?

Hi [Name],

I wanted to check whether [deliverable] is working as expected. Is there a specific issue or next step I can help clarify within our agreed scope?

Regards,  
[Name]

## Rules
- Stop immediately after an opt-out or clear no.
- Do not pretend to have a prior relationship.
- Never fabricate deadlines, availability, testimonials, or competing offers.
- Follow local marketing and privacy laws.
