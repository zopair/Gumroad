## Weekly production workflow
1. Research 10 real questions from customers, comments, support tickets, and search suggestions.
2. Select 5 topics that connect to one business objective.
3. Draft all hooks and CTAs before production.
4. Batch-create assets; adapt the idea to each platform rather than reposting blindly.
5. Review results by qualified actions, not likes alone.
