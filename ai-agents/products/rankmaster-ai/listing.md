## The problem

Writers often start drafting before they understand the reader's intent, page purpose, or evidence needed to make the content useful.

## What this product does

Organize a topic, audience, primary keyword, and up to 20 supplied secondary phrases into a structured SEO brief. RankMaster removes duplicate phrases, records the selected search intent, and maps the keyword cluster to title, metadata, headings, introduction, body, and FAQ placements.

## What is included

- SEO brief workflow
- Primary and secondary keyword cluster
- Search-intent classification
- Recommended title and meta description
- Heading and content placement map
- On-page editorial checklist
- Structured report output

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it provide live keyword volume or rankings?

No. The local edition does not query a live search provider or invent volume, difficulty, ranking, or SERP figures. Supply phrases from a licensed research source and validate demand separately.

### Does it guarantee rankings?

No. Rankings depend on competition, site quality, intent, links, and many external factors.

### Can it support Arabic?

It accepts Unicode text; native-language review is needed for wording and intent.
