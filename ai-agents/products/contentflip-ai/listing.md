## The problem

Useful insights in recordings are difficult to reuse when the transcript is unstructured and source context gets lost.

## What this product does

Organize supplied transcript text into an article structure and preserve basic source details for editorial review.

## What is included

- Transcript segmentation
- Article structure
- Markdown-ready draft
- Source traceability
- Editorial and rights checklist

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it transcribe audio or video?

Not in the local baseline. It transforms a transcript that you provide.

### Does it download videos?

No. Use only media you are authorized to access and process.

### Can the article publish without review?

Review quotations, facts, names, and permissions before publishing.
