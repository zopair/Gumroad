## The problem

Teams rewrite the same idea for multiple channels and lose consistency when review and approval are informal.

## What this product does

Prepare channel-specific draft copy, record the requested brand voice, and supply a human review checklist.

## What is included

- Channel-specific drafts
- Brand voice input
- Editorial review checklist
- Approval-first status
- Structured content pack

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it publish to accounts?

No. It creates drafts only; publishing and scheduling are not connected.

### Does it guarantee engagement?

No. Reach and engagement depend on audience, creative quality, timing, and platform factors.

### Can I add channels?

The workflow accepts channel names; each channel needs editorial and format review.
