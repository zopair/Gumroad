## The problem

Market commentary can blur measured observations, assumptions, and predictions, making it difficult to see what data supports.

## What this product does

Calculate descriptive statistics from supplied closing prices and separate observed metrics from scenario hypotheses.

## What is included

- Price-series validation
- Period change and average
- Return and volatility measures
- Scenario framework
- Risk disclosures

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it connect to an exchange?

No. This baseline analyzes price data that you provide.

### Does it tell me what to buy?

No. It provides descriptive analysis, not investment advice or trade signals.

### Can it place trades?

No. Live order execution is intentionally excluded.
