## The problem

Market commentary can blur measured observations, assumptions, and predictions, making it difficult to see what data supports.

## What this product does

Calculate descriptive statistics and technical indicators from supplied closing prices. It reports RSI (14), EMA (20), SMA (20) when enough observations exist, MACD (12, 26, 9) when sufficient history is available, maximum drawdown, and a historical 95% VaR estimate. Missing-history indicators are explicitly withheld rather than fabricated.

## What is included

- Price-series validation
- Period change, moving averages, and return statistics
- RSI and MACD technical indicators
- Historical volatility and maximum drawdown
- Historical 95% VaR and worst observed period return
- Bull/base/bear scenario framework
- Risk disclosures and data limitations

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it connect to an exchange?

No. This baseline analyzes price data that you provide and makes no live market-data request.

### Does it tell me what to buy?

No. It provides descriptive analysis, not investment advice or trade signals. Historical risk metrics are not forecasts or guarantees.

### Can it place trades?

No. Live order execution is intentionally excluded.
