**Tags:** B2B leads, prospect research, sales operations, lead qualification, CSV workflow

## The problem

Raw prospect lists are often duplicated, incomplete, and disconnected from the source that supports each company record.

## What this product does

Normalize supplied company records, validate source URLs, remove duplicates, and score record completeness with an explicit explanation.

## What is included

- Company record validation
- Duplicate detection
- Source URL provenance
- Completeness scoring
- Structured prospect report

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it scrape personal profiles?

No. The local workflow processes company-level records supplied by the user.

### Does a high score mean the lead will buy?

No. The score measures record completeness, not buying intent or verified fit.

### Does it verify emails?

No. Email discovery and verification are not included in this edition.
