# EstateGuard AI

> Spot clauses that deserve a closer human review.

**SEO title:** EstateGuard AI | Contract Clause Review Assistant  
**Meta description:** Flag common contract clause references and capture source excerpts for human review. A document triage aid, not legal advice.  
**Primary keyword:** contract clause review assistant  
**Tags:** contract review, real estate, lease analysis, legal operations, document triage

## The problem

Important contract terms can be buried in long documents, making first-pass review slow and inconsistent.

## What this product does

Identify common clause keywords, capture source line excerpts, and suggest focused questions for a qualified reviewer.

## What is included

- Deposit and payment flags
- Termination and renewal references
- Liability and penalty checks
- Source line excerpts
- Human review prompts

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it a legal adviser?

No. It provides document triage only and does not give legal advice.

### Does it determine enforceability?

No. Enforceability depends on the full contract and applicable jurisdiction.

### Does it read scanned PDFs?

The current runtime accepts extracted contract text; OCR is not included.
